### About me

I am a 6th year PhD student in computer science at Yale University and I am fortunate to be advised by Dan Spielman. Before then, I was an undergraduate at the University of Chicago, advised by Lorenzo Orecchia.

I am broadly interested in theoretical computer science, including graph theory, average-case complexity, spectral methods, Sum-of-Squares algorithms, and high-dimensional statistics. Recently I have become interested in finite free probability, numerical linear algebra, and the theory of language generation.

Previously, I participated in competitive programming. I am a GO player, and I was a member of UChicago GO Team.

Here is my [**CV**](https://xifanyu.github.io/files/CV-26-09.pdf).
